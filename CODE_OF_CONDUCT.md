# Code of Conduct

Voxriva welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience or
ability. This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces
where people participate on behalf of the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate and clear. Discuss ideas and code constructively.
- Give people room to ask questions and learn; accept actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only information you have permission to share.
- Help keep discussions focused, accessible and useful to other contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, doxxing, personal
attacks and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/AlexIvanchyk/Voxriva/issues) to request
moderator help. If details are sensitive, post only a request for private contact
and wait for a maintainer to arrange a private channel. Do not publish another
person's private information or sensitive incident details. GitHub's own reporting
and blocking tools are also available for behavior on its platform.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate project content, warn participants,
limit participation or exclude people depending on severity and repeated behavior.
They should explain decisions where practical while respecting the privacy of
those involved. The repository owner [@AlexIvanchyk](https://github.com/AlexIvanchyk)
coordinates moderation; no response-time guarantee is established.
